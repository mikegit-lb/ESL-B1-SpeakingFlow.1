# 🌟 ESL B1 SPEAKING MASTERCLASS: "BEHIND THE FILTER"
### *Identity, Algorithms, Mental Wellbeing & Everyday Heroes*

---

## 🎯 LESSON OVERVIEW
* **Level:** B1 (Intermediate)
* **Target Student:** 16-year-old high school student / teenager
* **Lesson Theme:** Exploring who we are online vs. in real life, navigating mental health and social media pressures, and redefining what true heroes look like in the 21st century.
* **Key Skills:** Fluency building, defending opinions, comparing & contrasting, giving advice, hypothetical thinking (Second Conditional), and self-reflection.
* **Estimated Time:** 60 – 90 minutes (or two 45-minute modular sessions)

# 📚 SECTION 1: MASTER VOCABULARY BANK (60+ Words & Phrases)
*Categorized with clear B1 definitions, parts of speech, and teen-relatable example sentences.*

### 📱 Theme A: Social Media & The Algorithm (15 Words)
1. **Curated feed** *(noun phrase)*: A social media page where all photos and posts are carefully selected and edited to look perfect.  
   *Example:* "Remember that her Instagram is just a *curated feed*, not her real 24/7 life."
2. **Doomscrolling** *(noun)*: The act of continuously scrolling through bad or stressful news and endless short videos.  
   *Example:* "I spent two hours *doomscrolling* on TikTok last night before bed."
3. **Digital footprint** *(noun phrase)*: The permanent trail of data, photos, and comments you leave behind on the internet.  
   *Example:* "Think twice before posting; your *digital footprint* can last forever."
4. **Influencer** *(noun)*: A person on social media who has the power to affect what other people buy or believe.  
   *Example:* "That beauty *influencer* gets paid thousands of dollars for a 30-second review."
5. **Authentic** *(adjective)*: Real, genuine, and not fake or staged.  
   *Example:* "I love creators who are *authentic* and show their bad days too."
6. **Clickbait** *(noun)*: Sensational or exaggerated video titles designed to make people click.  
   *Example:* "The video title was total *clickbait*; nothing crazy actually happened."
7. **Algorithm** *(noun)*: The computer formula that decides which videos and posts appear on your feed.  
   *Example:* "My YouTube *algorithm* knows I love gaming and cats."
8. **Sponsored post** *(noun)*: A post created because a company paid the creator to promote a product.  
   *Example:* "She didn't buy that energy drink—it was clearly a *sponsored post*."
9. **Engagement** *(noun)*: The amount of likes, shares, comments, and views a post gets.  
   *Example:* "Memes always get the highest *engagement* on my feed."
10. **Aesthetic** *(noun/adjective)*: An attractive visual style or vibe.  
    *Example:* "Her room has a very cozy, vintage *aesthetic*."
11. **Trendsetter** *(noun)*: A person who starts a new fashion, dance, or online trend.  
    *Example:* "He is a true *trendsetter*; everyone in school copied his jacket style."
12. **Shadowban** *(verb/noun)*: When a platform secretly hides someone's content without telling them.  
    *Example:* "My videos barely have 50 views; I think TikTok *shadowbanned* my account."
13. **Unfollow / Mute** *(verb)*: To stop seeing someone’s updates on social media.  
    *Example:* "If an account makes you feel insecure, just *unfollow* or *mute* them."
14. **Viral** *(adjective)*: Spreading extremely quickly across the internet.  
    *Example:* "His acoustic cover went *viral* overnight and hit 3 million views."
15. **To scroll endlessly** *(verb phrase)*: To swipe through posts without a clear goal or stopping point.  
    *Example:* "It's so easy *to scroll endlessly* when you're supposed to be studying."

### 🧠 Theme B: Personality Types & Traits (15 Words)
16. **Introvert** *(noun)*: A person who gets energized by spending time alone in quiet environments.  
    *Example:* "As an *introvert*, I need quiet time after a long day at school."
17. **Extrovert** *(noun)*: A lively person who gets energy from being around large groups of people.  
    *Example:* "My brother is an extreme *extrovert* who talks to everyone at the party."
18. **Ambivert** *(noun)*: A person who has a balance of both introvert and extrovert traits.  
    *Example:* "I'm an *ambivert*—I love parties, but I also love staying home on Sundays."
19. **Outspoken** *(adjective)*: Direct and fearless when expressing opinions.  
    *Example:* "She is very *outspoken* during class debates and isn't afraid to disagree."
20. **Overthinker** *(noun)*: Someone who worries and thinks too much about small details.  
    *Example:* "I am a total *overthinker*; I reread every text message five times before sending."
21. **Empathetic** *(adjective)*: Able to understand and feel another person's emotions.  
    *Example:* "A good best friend must be *empathetic* when you're going through a hard time."
22. **People-pleaser** *(noun)*: A person who always tries to make others happy, often ignoring their own needs.  
    *Example:* "Saying 'no' is hard when you're a *people-pleaser*."
23. **Easygoing** *(adjective)*: Relaxed, calm, and not easily stressed or upset.  
    *Example:* "Our group leader is very *easygoing*, so working with him is stress-free."
24. **Ambitious** *(adjective)*: Having a strong desire to achieve great success or goals.  
    *Example:* "She is very *ambitious* and wants to study medicine abroad."
25. **Charismatic** *(adjective)*: Having a magnetic personality that attracts and charms people.  
    *Example:* "The student council president is so *charismatic* when giving speeches."
26. **Self-conscious** *(adjective)*: Feeling nervous or uncomfortable about how you look or act in front of others.  
    *Example:* "I felt really *self-conscious* speaking in front of the whole grade."
27. **Reliable** *(adjective)*: Trustworthy; someone you can always count on.  
    *Example:* "Whenever I need help with homework, Max is always *reliable*."
28. **Spontaneous** *(adjective)*: Doing things suddenly based on impulse rather than planning.  
    *Example:* "Let's be *spontaneous* and take the train to the beach right now!"
29. **Adaptable** *(adjective)*: Able to adjust easily to new situations and changes.  
    *Example:* "Moving to a new high school requires you to be *adaptable*."
30. **Quirk** *(noun)*: An unusual or unique habit or personality detail.  
    *Example:* "One of her funny *quirks* is organizing pens by color shade."

### 🧘 Theme C: Mental Health & Self-Image (15 Words)
31. **Self-esteem** *(noun)*: How much you respect, value, and feel confident in yourself.  
    *Example:* "Getting compliments can boost your *self-esteem*, but true confidence comes from inside."
32. **FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)** *(noun)*: Anxiety that exciting things are happening without you.  
    *Example:* "Seeing everyone at the concert on Instagram gave me terrible *FOMO*."
33. **Digital detox** *(noun phrase)*: A period of time when you avoid using smartphones and computers.  
    *Example:* "I did a weekend *digital detox* and felt much calmer on Monday."
34. **Burnout** *(noun)*: Extreme physical and mental exhaustion caused by long-term stress or overwork.  
    *Example:* "Balancing exams, sports, and part-time tutoring led to total *burnout*."
35. **Peer pressure** *(noun)*: Feeling forced to do what others in your age group are doing to fit in.  
    *Example:* "He started wearing those expensive sneakers just because of *peer pressure*."
36. **Body image** *(noun)*: How you see and feel about your own physical appearance.  
    *Example:* "Comparing yourself to edited photos can damage your *body image*."
37. **Comparison trap** *(noun phrase)*: The unhealthy habit of comparing your life to others' highlight reels.  
    *Example:* "Don't fall into the *comparison trap* while browsing social media."
38. **Validation** *(noun)*: Proof or feeling that you are accepted, worthy, or liked.  
    *Example:* "Depending on social media likes for *validation* can be dangerous."
39. **To unplug** *(verb)*: To disconnect from digital devices and the internet.  
    *Example:* "It's healthy *to unplug* for at least one hour before going to sleep."
40. **Overwhelming** *(adjective)*: Feeling too intense, stressful, or hard to manage.  
    *Example:* "Having five tests in one week was completely *overwhelming*."
41. **Boundary** *(noun)*: A healthy limit you set to protect your peace and energy.  
    *Example:* "Setting a *boundary* like not replying to messages after 10 PM helped my sleep."
42. **Mindset** *(noun)*: A person’s general attitude and way of thinking.  
    *Example:* "A positive *mindset* makes difficult challenges easier to handle."
43. **Self-care** *(noun)*: Activities you do purposefully to protect your mental and physical health.  
    *Example:* "Going for a walk without my phone is my favorite form of *self-care*."
44. **Recharge** *(verb)*: To rest and recover your physical and mental energy.  
    *Example:* "I spent Sunday listening to music just to *recharge* for the week."
45. **Insecure** *(adjective)*: Not confident; full of doubts about oneself.  
    *Example:* "Most teenagers feel *insecure* about something, even if they hide it well."

### 🦸 Theme D: Heroes & Role Models (15 Words)
46. **Role model** *(noun)*: A person whose behavior and success serve as an inspiring example for others.  
    *Example:* "My older sister is my biggest *role model* because of her work ethic."
47. **To look up to someone** *(phrasal verb)*: To admire and respect someone deeply.  
    *Example:* "I really *look up to* athletes who use their fame to help charity."
48. **Integrity** *(noun)*: Doing what is morally right and honest, even when no one is watching.  
    *Example:* "A true leader shows *integrity* in every decision they make."
49. **Resilience** *(noun)*: The ability to bounce back quickly after failure or difficult times.  
    *Example:* "Her *resilience* after her injury inspired the whole volleyball team."
50. **Overcome obstacles** *(verb phrase)*: To conquer difficulties and keep going.  
    *Example:* "Great heroes are defined by how they *overcome obstacles*."
51. **Selfless** *(adjective)*: Caring more about other people's needs than one's own.  
    *Example:* "Volunteer firefighters perform *selfless* acts of courage every day."
52. **Trailblazer** *(noun)*: A pioneer who does something innovative and opens paths for others.  
    *Example:* "Marie Curie was a scientific *trailblazer* for women around the world."
53. **Make a difference** *(verb phrase)*: To have a meaningful, positive impact on the world.  
    *Example:* "You don't need millions of followers to *make a difference* in your community."
54. **Moral compass** *(noun phrase)*: An internal sense that guides you between right and wrong.  
    *Example:* "When making tough choices, always follow your *moral compass*."
55. **Advocate** *(noun/verb)*: Someone who publicly supports a cause or speaks up for others.  
    *Example:* "She is a passionate *advocate* for climate action and mental health awareness."
56. **Humble** *(adjective)*: Modest; not arrogant or bragging about achievements.  
    *Example:* "Despite winning the gold medal, the champion remained kind and *humble*."
57. **Courageous** *(adjective)*: Brave; willing to face danger or fear.  
    *Example:* "It takes a *courageous* person to stand up to a bully."
58. **Everyday hero** *(noun phrase)*: An ordinary person who does extraordinary, kind, or brave things.  
    *Example:* "Nurses and teachers are the true *everyday heroes* of society."
59. **Inspire** *(verb)*: To give someone the feeling and motivation to do something great.  
    *Example:* "His speech *inspired* me to start learning a new language."
60. **Superficial** *(adjective)*: Shallow; caring only about surface appearances rather than real depth.  
    *Example:* "Judging someone only by their follower count is very *superficial*."

### 🗣️ Conversational Power-Phrases (Opinion & Debate Scaffolds)
* **Expressing Opinion:** *"If you ask me..."* / *"From my perspective..."* / *"To be totally honest..."*
* **Agreeing & Adding:** *"I couldn't agree more, and what's more..."* / *"That's spot on!"*
* **Polite Disagreement:** *"I see your point, but on the other hand..."* / *"I'm not so sure about that because..."*
* **Stating Dilemmas:** *"I'm really torn between... and..."* / *"It depends on how you look at it."*
* **Hypothetical Thinking:** *"If I were in that situation, I would definitely..."*

# 💬 SECTION 2: 38 DYNAMIC SPEAKING QUESTIONS

### 🚀 Round 1: Quick-Fire Icebreakers (Warming Up)
1. What app on your phone has your highest screen time, and what are you usually doing on it?
2. If your personality were a music genre or a specific song, what would it be?
3. What is one trend online right now that you find funny, and one that you find totally cringe?
4. Are you someone who charges your social battery by being alone or by hanging out with friends?
5. Who is the first person that comes to mind when you hear the word *"hero"*?
6. If you could mute one phrase or word from the entire internet forever, what would it be?

---

### 📱 Round 2: Social Media, Algorithms & Influencer Culture
7. Do you think influencers actually live the lives they show on camera, or is 90% of it just *curated content*?
8. How does the *algorithm* influence the way teenagers dress, talk, and think nowadays?
9. Have you ever bought something or tried a restaurant purely because you saw it on a *sponsored post* or TikTok? Was it worth it?
10. Should influencers have a moral responsibility for what they promote to young audiences (e.g., energy drinks, fast fashion, crypto)?
11. If you woke up tomorrow with 1 million followers, what would your content be about, and how would you handle the hate comments?
12. Why do you think *clickbait* and dramatic gossip spread faster than positive or educational news?
13. Is the concept of a *"digital footprint"* taken seriously enough by people your age?

---

### 🧠 Round 3: Personality Types, Identity & Friendship Vibes
14. Which label fits you best: *introvert, extrovert, or ambivert*? Give an example of a situation where this shows.
15. Do you think a person's online persona (how they act on social media) matches their real-life personality? Why or why not?
16. In friendship groups, what personality role do you usually play (the funny one, the organizer, the listener, the *overthinker*, the rebel)?
17. What personality trait do you admire most in other people, and what is one trait you find hard to tolerate?
18. Do you think personality tests (like MBTI or Zodiac signs) are accurate, or are people just looking for a way to describe themselves?
19. Have you ever felt like a *people-pleaser*? In what situations is it hardest for you to say "no"?
20. How has your personality changed from when you were 12 years old to now at 16?

---

### 🧘 Round 4: Mental Health, Screen Habits & Self-Image
21. Have you ever experienced *FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)* while scrolling on a Friday night? How did you deal with it?
22. How do beauty filters and edited photos affect teenagers' *self-esteem* and *body image*?
23. What is your opinion on doing a 48-hour *digital detox*? Could you survive without your phone for an entire weekend?
24. When school or life gets *overwhelming*, what is your favorite way to *unplug* and *recharge* your mind?
25. Why do you think people care so much about getting *validation* (likes, views, comments) from strangers on the internet?
26. If you notice a friend is feeling down or experiencing *burnout*, what are some practical ways you can support them?
27. Do you think schools should teach a subject dedicated to mental wellbeing, stress management, and digital health?

---

### 🦸 Round 5: True Heroes vs. Internet Celebrities
28. What is the biggest difference between a famous *celebrity* and a genuine *role model*?
29. Who is an *everyday hero* in your personal life (a teacher, family member, friend, neighbor), and what makes them heroic?
30. Does a real hero need to be completely flawless, or do mistakes make a role model more relatable?
31. If you had to choose between being world-famous for 15 minutes of viral fame OR quietly making a huge difference in your local community, which would you pick?
32. What historical or modern figure has demonstrated great *resilience* and *courage* in your eyes?
33. Can fictional characters (from movies, anime, books, or games) be effective role models for teenagers? Who is your favorite?
34. In your opinion, what are the top three qualities that every great leader must possess?

---

### 🔥 Round 6: "Would You Rather?" & Spicy Dilemma Questions
35. **Would you rather** have unlimited screen time but have zero privacy (parents can see everything), OR have only 30 minutes of phone time per day with total privacy?
36. **Would you rather** be an extremely talented *introvert* who works behind the scenes, OR a famous, *charismatic extrovert* who is constantly in the public eye?
37. **Would you rather** post only 100% *authentic*, unfiltered photos of yourself with zero filters, OR never be allowed to post photos again?
38. **Would you rather** have a personal conversation with your biggest celebrity idol OR spend an afternoon getting advice from your future 30-year-old self?

# 🎮 SECTION 3: INTERACTIVE SPEAKING MISSIONS & GAMES

### 🕹️ Mission 1: "The Advice Columnist" (Roleplay Challenge)
*Student acts as the teen advice host "Doctor Real Talk". The teacher presents 3 listener dilemmas, and the student must give 3 pieces of advice using at least 2 target vocabulary words per answer.*

* **Case #1 (The Phone Zombie):**  
  *"Dear Doctor Real Talk, my best friend is always scrolling TikTok when we hang out. Even at lunch, they are glued to their screen. I feel invisible. What should I do?"*  
  *(Target words to try: boundary, unplug, outspoken, authentic)*

* **Case #2 (The Comparison Trap):**  
  *"Dear Doctor Real Talk, whenever I open Instagram, I see classmates at parties or influencers on luxury vacations. I feel like my life is boring and I hate how I look in photos."*  
  *(Target words to try: curated feed, self-esteem, FOMO, comparison trap)*

* **Case #3 (The Pressure Cooker):**  
  *"Dear Doctor Real Talk, I have basketball practice, violin lessons, exams, and I'm also trying to keep everyone happy. I'm exhausted and can't sleep."*  
  *(Target words to try: burnout, people-pleaser, recharge, overwhelming)*

---

### ⚖️ Mission 2: The 60-Second Mini-Debate Showdown
*Pick a topic. Give the student 30 seconds to brainstorm 2 points, then speak for 60 seconds using conversational power-phrases.*

* **Debate 1:** *"Social media platforms should ban all beauty and face-altering filters for users under 18."*
* **Debate 2:** *"Real-life teachers and healthcare workers should be paid more and celebrated more than athletes and YouTubers."*
* **Debate 3:** *"Introverts make better, more thoughtful leaders than extroverts."*

---

### 🏆 Mission 3: The "Everyday Superhero Award" (Creative Pitch)
*The student must nominate ONE real person they know (or know of) for the "Everyday Superhero Award."*
* **Instructions:** Give a 2-minute presentation answering:
  1. Who is the nominee and how do you know them?
  2. What *obstacles* have they overcome?
  3. What *personality traits* make them special (*resilient, humble, empathetic*)?
  4. Why do you *look up to* them more than an internet celebrity?
