Roleplay Mission

📻 Dr. Real-Talk Podcast

The student acts as a famous teen advice podcaster. Read the letter, give 3 pieces of advice using at least 2 target vocab words.

Letter #1: The Phone Zombie

"My best friend is constantly doomscrolling TikTok even when we have lunch together. I feel invisible..."

Letter #2: The Comparison Trap

"Everyone's feeds look like luxury vacations and perfect lives. I feel my life is boring and I hate photos of myself..."

Letter #3: The Pressure Cooker

"I have basketball, piano lessons, exams, and I try to make everyone happy. I'm burnt out and can't sleep..."

🎯 Recommended Vocab: boundary, unplug, authentic

Presentation Pitch

🏆 Everyday Superhero Award

Nominate ONE real person in your life for the annual Everyday Superhero Medal. Deliver a 2-minute pitch following these 4 checkpoints:

  • 1 Who is your hero? (Family member, teacher, local worker, doctor, friend?)
  • 2 Obstacles: What difficult challenge or hardship have they overcome?
  • 3 Personality: Which 3 traits make them special (*resilient, humble, selfless*)?
  • 4 Comparison: Why do they inspire you more than a millionaire TikToker?
🎯 Goal: 2 Minutes Speaking
Section 4

Conversational Power-Phrases

Use these sentence starters to sound more natural, spontaneous, and confident in B1 discussions.

💬 Expressing Opinions
  • "If you ask me..."
  • "To be totally honest with you..."
  • "From my perspective as a teenager..."
  • "The way I look at it is..."
🤝 Agreeing & Expanding
  • "I couldn't agree more, and plus..."
  • "That's spot on! In fact..."
  • "That's completely relatable because..."
  • "You took the words right out of my mouth!"
🤔 Polite Disagreeing
  • "I see your point, but on the other hand..."
  • "I'm not so sure about that because..."
  • "That might be true for some, however..."
  • "I look at it a bit differently..."
🔮 Hypothetical (Conditionals)
  • "If I were in their shoes, I would..."
  • "If I had to pick between the two..."
  • "Imagine if nobody used social media..."
  • "Supposing that happened to me, I’d..."
